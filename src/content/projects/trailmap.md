## What it does

Trailmap lets you load GPX tracks, see them on a vector map, and read an elevation
profile that follows your cursor. Tiles are cached so the map keeps working with
no signal.

## Highlights

- Service worker caches tiles for any region you have viewed.
- Elevation profile is rendered on a canvas and stays smooth with 50k-point tracks.
- Imports and exports GPX with no server involved.
